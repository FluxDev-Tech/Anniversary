🎁 Setup Your Gift Website

Follow these simple steps to add your special photo!

1

Prepare Your Image

Choose a special photo of you and your girlfriend:

  • Clear, high-quality image
  • Good lighting and recognizable faces
  • Square or close to square ratio works best
  • At least 600x600 pixels
  • JPG or PNG format

💡 Pro Tip:

Pick a photo from a meaningful moment - first date, favorite memory, or just a beautiful candid shot!

2

Rename Your Image

Rename your photo file to exactly:

puzzle-image.jpg

❌ Wrong Names:

  • • IMG_1234.jpg
  • • my photo.jpg
  • • Puzzle-Image.jpg
  • • puzzle_image.png

✅ Correct Name:

  • • puzzle-image.jpg
  • (all lowercase)
  • (hyphen, not underscore)
  • (exactly as shown)
3

Place in Correct Folder

Move your renamed image to the website folder:

Your folder should look like this:

📁 anniversary-website/
📄 index.html
📄 styles.css
📄 script.js
🖼️ puzzle-image.jpg ← YOUR IMAGE!
📄 README.md

⚠️ Important:

The image MUST be in the same folder as index.html - not in a subfolder!

4

Test It!

Open the website and check if your image loads:

  1. Double-click index.html
  2. Enter password: Adi or 2004
  3. Wait for puzzle to load
  4. You should see your image split into puzzle pieces!

✅ Success!

If you see your photo in the puzzle pieces, you're all set! The website is ready to share with your girlfriend.

❌ See a Heart?

If you see a heart placeholder instead of your photo, check: filename, location, and spelling. Refer to IMAGE-SETUP-GUIDE.md
